Our story ਸਾਡਾ ਇਤਿਹਾਸ

Carrying forward the spirit of the Singh Sabha movement

In 1873, the Singh Sabha movement began in Amritsar to renew Sikh learning, reform society and open schools across Punjab. Its legacy built many of the institutions Punjab still relies on.

Vision ਦ੍ਰਿਸ਼ਟੀ

A drug-free, educated and self-reliant youth of Punjab, living by Gurmat and serving all of humanity.

Mission ਮਿਸ਼ਨ

To organise young people in every pind and ward of Punjab to learn, serve and lead: by spreading Sikh values, supporting education and livelihoods, fighting addiction, caring for health and the environment, and standing with anyone in need, regardless of faith or caste.

How we are organised ਢਾਂਚਾ

From the state body to every pind

Decisions are made by an elected executive and carried out by local units, so programmes stay close to the people they serve.

Alongside the nine departments, four support cells keep the Sabha running: Media & IT, Legal Aid & RTI, Finance & Audit, and Membership & Units.
