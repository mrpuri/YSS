Who we are ਸਾਡੇ ਬਾਰੇ

A movement of young Sikhs and friends, organised for lasting change.

We are a society registered in Punjab, run by and for young people. Our members keep alive the spirit of the original Singh Sabha movement — learning, reform and service — and apply it to today's challenges: drugs, unemployment, migration, and the environment.

Every programme is planned through one of our nine departments and delivered by local units in villages and wards, so help reaches people where they live.
