# Youth Singh Sabha — proposed content structure

A registered society in Punjab, so the site has three jobs:

1. **Credibility** — show it is a real, registered, accountable body (registration, committee, audited accounts).
2. **Participation** — make it easy for young people to join, volunteer, or open a unit in their own pind/ward.
3. **Support** — make it easy for the sangat (in Punjab and abroad) to donate and follow the work.

Everything is bilingual in spirit: English body copy with Gurmukhi headings, so it reads naturally to both local youth and the diaspora.

## Departments (ਵਿਭਾਗ)

Chosen for what matters most to youth in Punjab today: Sikh identity, education & jobs, the drug crisis, health, floods/relief, sports, environment (parali, groundwater) and women's participation.

| # | Department | Gurmukhi | Focus | Flagship programmes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dharam Parchar** (Gurmat & Heritage) | ਧਰਮ ਪ੍ਰਚਾਰ | Sikh values, history, Gurbani | Gurmat camps, kirtan & santhya classes, Sikh history quiz, dastar sikhlai camps |
| 2 | **Sikhya** (Education & Careers) | ਸਿੱਖਿਆ | Learning and career guidance | Free evening tuition, scholarships, career counselling, competitive-exam & IELTS guidance, pind libraries |
| 3 | **Nasha Mukt Punjab** (De-addiction) | ਨਸ਼ਾ ਮੁਕਤ ਪੰਜਾਬ | Prevention, counselling, recovery support | School/college awareness, peer counsellors, referral to govt. de-addiction centres, family support groups |
| 4 | **Sehat** (Health & Blood Donation) | ਸਿਹਤ | Community health | Blood donation camps, blood-donor registry, free medical & eye camps, mental-health first aid |
| 5 | **Sewa & Bhalai** (Welfare & Relief) | ਸੇਵਾ ਤੇ ਭਲਾਈ | Social welfare and disaster response | Flood relief, ration for needy families, support for elderly & widows, langar sewa |
| 6 | **Khed & Gatka** (Sports) | ਖੇਡ ਤੇ ਗੱਤਕਾ | Fitness and discipline as an alternative to addiction | Gatka akhara, kabaddi & athletics tournaments, pind gyms, fitness drives |
| 7 | **Rozgar te Hunar** (Skills & Employment) | ਰੁਜ਼ਗਾਰ ਤੇ ਹੁਨਰ | Livelihoods without needing to migrate | Skill training, job fairs, digital literacy, start-up & self-employment mentoring |
| 8 | **Vatavaran** (Environment) | ਵਾਤਾਵਰਣ | "Pavan Guru, Pani Pita, Mata Dharat Mahat" | Tree plantation, parali-burning alternatives, water conservation, clean-pind drives |
| 9 | **Kaur Wing** (Women's Wing) | ਕੌਰ ਵਿੰਗ | Leadership and safety for young women | Self-defence (gatka) for girls, health & hygiene camps, leadership training, skill groups |

**Support cells** (not public-facing departments, listed on About page): Media & IT, Legal Aid & RTI help, Finance & Audit, Membership & Units.

## Organisation structure

- **General Body** — all registered members, meets annually (AGM)
- **Executive Committee** — President, Sr. Vice President, General Secretary, Joint Secretary, Treasurer, Department Heads
- **District Committees** — one per district of Punjab
- **Pind / Ward Units** — the base; minimum 11 members to start a unit

## Content the society needs to supply (placeholders marked `[ ]` on the site)

- Registration number, date, and Act (e.g. Societies Registration Act, 1860), registered office address
- 12A / 80G status and numbers (only claim if granted); FCRA status (needed before taking foreign donations)
- Founding year and short founding story
- Executive committee names, roles, photos
- Real impact numbers (members, units, blood units collected, camps held)
- Bank account / UPI ID for donations
- Phone numbers, email, social handles
- Photos of real programmes (replace the patterned image placeholders)
- Audited accounts and annual report PDFs
